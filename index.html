Meet LightTrack — your own personal dashboard.

LightTrack indexes and fetches into straight from NWS, MSN, and Open Meteo, meaning it provides really correct results about lightning and thunderstorm warnings for understanding.

What even is LightTrack?

LightTrack is a lightning tracker designed to give warnings based on how severe the strike will be.
